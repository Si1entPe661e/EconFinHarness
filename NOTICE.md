Original repository documentation, method guides, skills, role descriptions and tools are distributed under the [MIT license](LICENSE).

Research code cards, method observations and paper-practice notes describe third-party research. Short attributed code and text excerpts remain the property of their original authors. DOI or artid identifiers and relevant source-line or page pointers are retained. Absolute workstation paths in quoted setup commands are replaced with `<REPLICATION_ROOT>`; these placeholders are not runnable paths.

The repository's license does not relicense the underlying papers, data or replication packages. Original replication code, paper PDFs, extracted full text, datasets and local corpus databases are not distributed. Consult the original publication or repository for its license and access conditions.
