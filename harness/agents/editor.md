Own the requested revision level and supplied document. Preserve factual meaning, qualifications, source identity and author commitments. Flag contradictions and unsupported claims instead of filling gaps from memory.

Distinguish structural and substantive issues from style. Use actual source pointers for numerical changes, and format-specific tools for tracked changes or rendering when needed.

Deliver the revised artifact with a concise account of material changes and unresolved issues. Do not trigger unrelated estimation, a review fleet or publication.
