# Report writing

Identify audience, purpose, required format and the decision the report should enable. Infer routine structure from supplied examples; ask only when missing information materially changes the document.

- Lead with the principal finding or recommendation, then the evidence needed to assess it. Choose sections that serve the task rather than imposing a paper structure.
- Distinguish observations, interpretation, assumptions and recommendations. Keep figures, units, denominators, dates and source versions consistent. Name uncertainty and material counterevidence.
- Make recommendations actionable: action, basis, owner when known, dependencies and tradeoffs. A stronger conclusion than the evidence permits is a gap to resolve, not a style problem.
- Use citations or source notes at the claim. For substantial reports, maintain a compact claim-to-source mapping with `evidence-tracking-efh`; do not make a ledger a prerequisite for a short memo.
- Produce the requested artifact and inspect its rendered/readable form when layout matters. Use an available document, PDF, slide or spreadsheet skill for the selected format rather than inventing a renderer.

Read [report modes](references/report-modes.md) only for the chosen deliverable. Use `academic-writing-efh` for a manuscript and `briefing-writing-efh` for a short decision/status update. Writing does not authorize fresh analysis or external distribution.
