# Logic and expression categories

Each entry gives the signals that suggest a candidate, the test that decides it, the fix and the legitimate uses to keep. Signals locate candidates; the test decides. Every fix obeys the skill's integrity limits: a restated position, citation or magnitude comes from the draft, the supplied sources or a verified literature record, otherwise it is `[author: …]`.

## Argument integrity

### 1. Straw man (稻草人)

- Signals: an opponent's view stated without quotation or citation; extreme quantifiers in a paraphrase ("they assume markets are always efficient"); "so-called", "nothing more than"; gap claims about prior work ("no prior study", "the literature has ignored", "existing work assumes", "fails to account for"); 所谓……论者、无非是、这种观点认为（未引用）、现有研究忽视了、尚未有研究、以往文献均假设.
- Test: compare the paraphrase with the cited source or the user's actual words. A straw man changes scope, strength, conditions or the claim itself. For a gap claim, check that the cited work actually makes or omits what is claimed.
- Fix: restate the position as the source states it, with a quotation or page pointer, then respond to that version. If the source is unavailable, mark `author input`. If the response no longer works against the accurate version, delete the rebuttal; mark the row `fixed` when the restatement and any replacement link come from the source, and `author input` when the new positioning needs a fact the sources lack.
- Keep: an accurate, sourced paraphrase, including a blunt one or one that states the same condition, owner, date or consequence in other words; leave it unchanged. An attribution error the text does not rebut is reported under sycophancy (category 5).

### 2. Refuting an unmade claim (反驳空气)

- Signals: "One might object that…", "A common misconception is…", "It is often assumed…", "Contrary to popular belief…"; 有人可能会说、常见的误解是、很多人以为、与通常的看法相反.
- Test: decide from the supplied materials (the user's request, referee comments, the draft and its own citations) who holds the position. It is refuting air when no one in those materials holds it, when it is a generic or popular position that does not bear on this paper's claim, or when the text attributes to a named person a position their own words in the materials reject. A recognized threat to this paper's estimand or design (confounding, anticipation, an alternative mechanism, measurement, selection) is never refuting air.
- Fix: delete the rebuttal framing and state the positive claim; for a misattribution, restate the person's actual position or delete it. Keep a generic objection only if the author supplies a citation. If the draft answers a recognized threat only by assertion, keep the threat, mark the answer `author input` (needed: a test, design feature or source) and, if no answer exists, state the threat once as a limitation. Never turn the assertion into a positive claim.
- Keep: responses to referee comments; documented debates with citations; recognized threats to this paper's estimand or design with their answers, stated once; a misreading guard when the paper's own result plausibly invites that misreading; in reports, statements of what a check or completion does not establish.

### 3. Internal contradiction (前后矛盾)

- Signals: the same quantity reported with different values; a term redefined; the sample or estimand changing between sections; "causes" in one place and "is associated with" in another for the same estimate; a mechanism ruled out and later invoked; a decision or date stated two ways.
- Test: for each entry in the argument map, search later sections for statements that use it as a premise and confirm they agree. Cite both map entries for each contradiction found.
- Fix: correct to the version the sources support and cite the source. If the sources do not decide, mark both locations `author input` and keep both values. When one estimate is described causally in one place and associationally in another, use the causal wording only if the draft's identification section claims causal identification for that estimate; otherwise use the associational wording at both locations and mark the row `fixed`, citing the design section or plan. Mark `author input` only when neither states what the design identifies. A deliberate change, such as a different sample, needs an explicit sentence explaining it.
- Keep: reported disagreement between sources, explained as such.

### 4. Incoherent logic (逻辑断裂)

- Signals: connectives that assert an absent inference ("therefore", "thus", 因此, 由此可见 after an unrelated fact); "Moreover", "In addition", 此外, 同时 chains that list without building; a paragraph with no topic sentence.
- Test: state the paragraph's claim in one sentence. Each sentence must support, qualify, illustrate or apply that claim, and each consecutive pair must have an honest relation: cause, evidence, an example of the preceding claim, contrast, consequence, condition, or temporal or procedural sequence. A sentence that is only on the same topic fails.
- Fix: state the missing premise from the draft's own material (a premise is the draft's own only when the draft states that link; using a figure as support for a claim it was not offered for is a new premise, so mark `author input` and name the evidence needed), reorder, merge, or cut sentences that do not serve the paragraph's claim. Cut a whole paragraph when the argument map shows that no later claim, option or recommendation uses its content and it reports no estimate, test, diagnostic or comparison; this is a local fix. Removing a reported result is `proposed`. In a report or briefing, a descriptive figure or unquantified claim that no claim, option or recommendation uses is not a reported estimate, and a link the draft does not state is not a use: cut it as a local fix. Under a line-editing request, paragraph deletion and reordering are `proposed`. Do not invent a premise.
- Keep: deliberate lists, such as data descriptions, introduced as lists.

### 5. Sycophancy (谄媚)

- Signals: "You're absolutely right", "Great question", "As you astutely note", "This is a very important insight", "We thank the referee for this extremely insightful comment"; 你说得非常对、这是一个非常重要的问题、正如你敏锐地指出; adopting the requester's, referee's or decision-maker's conclusion and then stretching the evidence to fit it; a memo tilted toward the option its commissioner prefers; attributing to someone a broader or different claim than they made.
- Test: is each agreement backed by evidence in the text? Does each attribution match the person's actual words? Does the text concede a referee's or commissioner's position that the evidence does not support?
- Fix: delete praise; replace agreement with the evidence and its actual strength; restore each attributed position to what was said; state disagreement where the evidence requires it.
- Keep: one conventional courtesy per response-letter item ("We thank the referee for this suggestion") when the agreement that follows is supported, and plain acknowledgment of a verified point ("We agree and have added…").

### 6. Parroting (复读)

- Signals: "In other words", "Put differently", "What this means is", "You have actually identified a crucial issue…", "This reveals that…" followed by a restatement; 换句话说、也就是说、你实际上指出了一个非常重要的问题; a discussion paragraph that only re-describes results.
- Test: both conditions hold: (a) the passage adds no inference, evidence, condition, implication or counterexample beyond its source or the preceding text; and (b) it is framed as the writer's analysis or insight, or sits where analysis is expected.
- Fix: delete, or replace with the analysis the restatement was standing in for, if the draft supports it.
- Keep: clearly attributed summaries of sources in literature reviews and background; restated definitions or assumptions a later step uses; a brief restatement that frames the analysis that follows; summaries the genre requires, such as an abstract, an executive summary, the results preview in an introduction and the conclusion's one restatement of the takeaway.

## Expression discipline

### 7. Reflexive rhetorical symmetry (文体过度对称)

- Signals: "not A but B", "It's not X; it's Y", "on the one hand… on the other hand", "on the surface… in reality", "less about X than about Y", "both an opportunity and a challenge", reflexive triads; 不是 A，而是 B、一方面……另一方面……、表面上……实际上……、看似……实则……、与其说……不如说……、这不仅仅是……更是…….
- Test: for "not A but B", does anyone in the materials hold A? For "on the one hand… on the other", are the two sides in real tension and both needed? For a triad, does the content have exactly three items? Also flag a frame that recurs through a section even when each instance is defensible.
- Fix: state B directly, or keep one paired form where the contrast is the point.
- Keep: real distinctions, such as two estimands, two mechanisms or a stated alternative hypothesis; ordinary coordination such as "both firm and year fixed effects".

### 8. Intensifier overuse (过度使用级别修饰)

- Signals: very, extremely, fundamentally, truly, highly, deeply, profoundly, essentially, crucially, vital, remarkable, unprecedented, dramatic, "significant" without a test; 非常、极其、根本、真正、高度、深刻、本质上、彻底、极大、全面、至关重要、显著（非统计含义）.
- Test: can this degree be verified from the evidence? Is there a number in the draft that says it better?
- Fix: delete the intensifier, replace it with a magnitude the draft reports, or reserve "significant" for its statistical sense. If the sentence without the intensifier still asserts an unverified degree ("determines", "depends on", 取决于), narrow the verb to the relation the argument needs ("constrains", 受……约束) or mark `author input` for a source. Do not supply a magnitude the draft lacks.
- Keep: a degree the evidence supports; technical uses such as "highly correlated (ρ = 0.92)"; "economically significant" with a stated magnitude and benchmark; proper names and policy titles (全面注册制, 全面深化改革); noun uses such as 根本原因.

### 9. Unwarranted anthropomorphism (无端拟人化)

- Signals: "the data tell us", "the market decided to punish", "the algorithm wants", "the policy seeks to", "history teaches"; 数据告诉我们、市场惩罚了、模型认为、算法想要、制度试图.
- Test: does the abstraction have intent or agency? Is the usage a settled convention of the field?
- Fix: name the actual agent or mechanism: "prices fell after the announcement", "the estimator assigns more weight to…", "regulators designed the policy to…".
- Keep: field conventions ("the model predicts", "the estimator assumes"); people and institutions as actors ("investors expect", "the Fed raised rates", "firms respond"); market-implied expectations derived from prices; figures the text marks as figures.

### 10. Excessive metaphor (过度比喻)

- Signals: double-edged sword, engine of growth, tip of the iceberg, landscape, lens, tapestry, ecosystem, moat, catalyst, puzzle piece; 双刃剑、引擎、催化剂、钥匙、拼图、生态、灯塔、护城河、冰山一角; two or more figures in one paragraph; mixed metaphors.
- Test: what is the literal mechanism? Does the figure add anything the literal statement lacks?
- Fix: state the mechanism literally. Keep at most one deliberate figure where it aids understanding.
- Keep: established technical terms that began as metaphors, such as bubble, liquidity, contagion, shock and spillover.

### 11. Native-speaker idioms and slang (母语者俚语)

- Signals: move the needle, low-hanging fruit, game changer, silver bullet, at the end of the day, ballpark, no-brainer, kick the can down the road, a perfect storm; 硬核、躺平、内卷（口语用法）、破防、拉满、降维打击、yyds.
- Test: would a non-native specialist reader understand it immediately and precisely? Does the venue's register allow it?
- Fix: replace with the literal meaning: "has a measurable effect", "the easiest improvements", "a solution to every case".
- Keep: idioms that are also field terms, and quoted speech.

### 12. Excessive technical terminology (过度技术化名词)

- Signals: acronyms not defined at first use; field jargon in a general-audience report; terms borrowed loosely from other fields, such as paradigm, ontology, entropy, quantum or dimension; business buzzwords such as synergize or leverage as a verb; 范式、维度、闭环、颗粒度、底层逻辑、赋能、抓手.
- Test: does this audience need the term? Is it defined at first use? Does a plain word carry the same meaning?
- Fix: use the plain word, or define the term once and use it consistently. Replace borrowed jargon with the precise concept.
- Keep: standard terms of the field for a specialist audience, such as endogeneity, heterogeneous treatment effects and two-way fixed effects, defined where the venue expects it.

### 13. Filler (废话)

- Signals: "It is well known that", "In today's rapidly changing world", "As we all know", "This section will discuss", "In conclusion" followed by repetition, "It is worth noting that", offers of further work and closing pleasantries ("Next, we can…", "If you'd like, I can…", "Let me know if…", "Hope this helps"); 众所周知、随着……的快速发展、在当今社会、值得注意的是、值得一提的是、综上所述（重复前文）、接下来我们可以……、如需，我可以进一步……、希望对你有所帮助.
- Test: delete it. If the argument, evidence, scope statements and limitations, and necessary reader guidance survive intact, it was filler.
- Fix: delete, or replace with the specific claim it postponed.
- Keep: signposting a long document needs, such as a roadmap paragraph in a long introduction; genre-required summaries (see Parroting). Offers of further work and pleasantries are never document signposting.

## Interaction with the defensive pass

The defensive pass removes hedges and states advantages; this pass removes inflation. A sentence can fail both ways: "This truly groundbreaking method may potentially improve accuracy" needs the intensifier and the stacked hedge removed and the draft's number added. When the two checks conflict, the evidence decides the strength of the claim.

The conclusion's single restatement of the main takeaway, which defensive principle 9 requires, is not filler or parroting; remove only second and later restatements. Ownership follows what a sentence does. Disclaimers about the author's own claims, including a "not A but B" whose A is the author's own possible claim, belong to the defensive pass; objection-and-rebuttal sentences, ritual signposting and a "not A but B" whose A belongs to others or is decorative belong here. In check-only mode both passes read the original text: omit a row whose span and fix the defensive table already cover, cite that row, and still report logic categories in that span. In a logic-only run, a draft note that proposes dropping or altering a result is reported with the defensive type `Draft note`.
