# Check defensive writing and logic

Use this prompt after drafting, or on any supplied draft. Replace the bracketed parts.

> Run the revise module in harness/workflows/revise-writing.md on [file or pasted text], written for [audience or venue]. The central contribution is [contribution, or "identify it from the draft"]. [Optional sources: my original request, referee comments, cited papers, the research plan.] [Structural changes: apply to the abstract, introduction and conclusion / propose only.]
>
> Apply `defensive-writing-check-efh`. Treat the paper as a product launch, not a progress report: organize around the strongest supported contribution; report findings rather than process; remove self-weakening wording such as 遗憾的是, self-belittling 仅, 仍明显落后 and 效果有限 (keep 仅 that restricts where or for whom a result holds), and do not summarize weaknesses for the referee; lead with comparisons on an objective stated before the results and report the standard benchmark beside them as a tradeoff, never choosing comparisons by which numbers look best; describe unfavorable results by conditions and size, and explain them by objective differences or tradeoffs only where the draft supports it, otherwise ask me; state advantages explicitly; give every experiment an argumentative job; open with problem, gap, idea and the central result; end on the main takeaway. Point out every instance and give its revision.
>
> Apply `logic-check-efh`, after the defensive check when both run: straw men, rebuttals of claims nobody made, internal contradictions, sentences that do not advance the argument, sycophancy, parroting, reflexive "not A but B" symmetry, unverified intensifiers, unwarranted anthropomorphism, excessive metaphor, native-speaker idioms and slang, unneeded technical terms and filler.
>
> Then run the fact comparison. Do not change numbers, samples, specifications, estimands, outcome definitions, benchmarks, citations, significance statements or warranted uncertainty; do not remove identification diagnostics, pre-specified analyses or unfavorable results that bear on the conclusion; do not delete a limitation a reader needs (keep each once in its section); do not drop tried alternatives or their outcomes; do not state a cause the draft, its tables or sources do not establish; do not upgrade association to causation. Return the revised text, each check's findings and instance table, and the items that need my input. [Optional: check only, returning the findings and tables without rewriting.]

To run one check, delete the other check's paragraph.
