# Recommended rules: `bunching` (draft)

Written by the empirical-analyst role from the bunching method literature listed under "Method sources" and from two generated layers of corpus practice. The evidence base is thin. The observed layer (`observed.md`) holds 3 recipe cards (aer_111_4_3, ecta_2025_ecta22303, restud_2026_restud_rdag037), all with `partial` coverage, 8 specifications in total, no design variants and no specification-to-output links; `observed.md` itself states that with fewer than 5 papers shares are not meaningful and the records are pointers only. The reported layer (`reported.md`) holds 5 paper-practice notes, all 5 with a linked recipe card, but only 3 of those cards are in the bunching observed layer (the cards linked to aer_112_10_8 and restud_2026_restud_rdag071 are not distilled here, so their code practice is not visible in this method). No hold-out list applies to bunching. Observed and reported frequencies are evidence of practice, not of correctness; there is no majority threshold, and a tag absent from a note or a null card field means "not recorded", not "not done". Because the corpus is small, the rules below rest on the method sources, and the corpus pointers show where a rule can be seen in practice or where practice and rule diverge. Each rule states when it applies, its exceptions, its sources with verification status, corpus pointers (artid + record id and path:lines from `observed.md`; artid + page + locator from `reported.md`; `_all:` prefix for `harness/paradigms/_all/reported.md`) and the validation result. The document stays `draft` until its rules have been through an independent check and the open questions below are settled; check records live in `harness_build/pilot/runs/`.

## Applicability

- Covered: bunching at a kink or a notch in a single running variable with a known threshold (tax and benefit schedules, eligibility or collateral cutoffs, statutory ages, regulatory quantity thresholds); a counterfactual density built from a polynomial fit to binned counts with an excluded region; excess mass, missing mass and the mapping from excess mass to an elasticity; difference-in-bunching and cross-threshold variants in which the bunching statistic is compared across groups, thresholds or periods; bunching used to measure manipulation or discretion (reported quantities, scores) rather than a behavioural elasticity, with the elasticity rules dropped.
- Not covered / different branch: regression discontinuity or regression kink estimates at the same threshold (`rdd`), density tests used as RD validity checks (`rdd` r3), structural models estimated by matching bunching moments (`structural_estimation`; here only the statement of which bunching moments enter is in scope), bunching in two running variables, and dynamic bunching with repeated thresholds over time (no rule below addresses it).

## Reported practice behind the rules (paper text)

Counts are papers out of the 5 notes in `reported.md`; a note that lacks a tag did not record it. Pointers are artid, page range and locator as printed in `reported.md`; re-read the page before citing. With 5 notes every count is a pointer, not a frequency.

- r1 (kink or notch, target quantity, smooth counterfactual, local population): 4/5 notes record `identifying_assumption_explicit`, 3/5 `estimand_named`, 4/5 `design_variant_named` and 1/5 `other:smooth_counterfactual_density_argued`. Pointers: aer_111_4_3 p.13-14 Section II.B; aer_111_4_3 p.13-20 Section II.B and Section III.B, equations (3) and (5); ecta_2025_ecta22303 p.14-15 Section 3.2. Practice diverges from the rule in that only one note records the smooth-counterfactual assumption in so many words; the other papers state design-specific assumptions instead, such as which reference point is assumed (aer_112_10_8 p.9-23 Section I.B footnote; Section III.C Fact 3) or independence of loss size within the bunching region (ecta_2025_ecta22303 p.17-18 Section 3.3). One paper in the layer uses bunching as evidence for a mechanism inside a structural design rather than as the main estimator (concordance estimator paper_only 2), so the rule's "every bunching estimate" scope is wider than what the notes were written to record.
- r2 (bin width, polynomial order, excluded region, integration constraint, sensitivity grid): 1/5 notes record `other:bunching_counterfactual` (aer_112_10_8 p.22-23 Figure 6; Section III.C), 3/5 `running_variable_definition` (ecta_2025_ecta22303 p.15-15 Section 3.2, Equation 6) and 1/5 `winsorization` (ecta_2025_ecta22303 p.8-8 Table I note). One reader gap states that the polynomial degree and bandwidth used for a traditional bunching counterfactual are not given numeric values in the text. Practice diverges from the rule: the papers describe the counterfactual in words and show it in figures, no note records the bin width, order and window as a set, and in the concordance the bandwidth item is paper_only 1, code_only 1, unknown 1 and agree 1, so neither layer alone records the parameters the rule asks for. One paper goes beyond the rule by comparing three independently constructed counterfactuals side by side (ecta_2025_ecta22303 p.18-20 Sections 3.4-3.5; ecta_2025_ecta22303 p.20-21 Section 3.5, Figure 7).
- r3 (raw histogram, placebo, round numbers, measurement of the running variable): 3/5 notes record `raw_data_plot` (aer_111_4_3 p.1-1 Figure 1, Panel A; restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.8-12 Table 1, Table 2, Figure 1), 2/5 `density_test`, 1/5 `placebo_outcomes`, 1/5 `pre_trends_plot`, 1/5 `other:reform_based_placebo_check` (aer_111_4_3 p.24-26 Section IV.A-B), 2/5 `alt_sample` and 2/5 `measurement_error_discussed` (aer_111_4_3 p.11-11 Section I.D, footnote). A round-number check is recorded for 1/5 (aer_112_10_8 p.24-24 Section III.E, Bunching robustness paragraph). Practice diverges from the rule in the placebo device: the papers use a pre-reform bin share (restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.22-23 Section 5.3, Figure A11), a reform phase-in or a group without the policy, and no note records placebo thresholds along the support; one reader gap notes the absence of a formal density manipulation test for the running variable, which the rule does not require either.
- r4 (comparison group, stable counterfactual difference, normalisation, weights): 1/5 notes record `other:multi_discontinuity_bunching` (aer_111_4_3 p.13-13 Section II.B), 1/5 `other:excess_mass_normalization` (aer_111_4_3 p.20-20 Section III.B, equation (5)), 1/5 `parallel_trends_argued` (ecta_2025_ecta22303 p.15-16 Section 3.2, Figure 6), 4/5 `notes_state_sample` (the weighting scheme is stated in aer_111_4_3 p.12-20 Table 2 and Table 4 notes) and 3/5 `unit_of_observation_stated` (aer_111_4_3 p.11-12 Section I.D, Table 2). Practice diverges from the cards rather than from the rule: the comparison group is named in the text of both difference designs but the card field is null in both (concordance comparison_group paper_only 2), and no note records an unweighted variant of a weighted threshold-level regression.
- r5 (bootstrap at the dependence level, two-step uncertainty, few groups, notes state the resampling level): 2/5 notes record `no_justification_found` (aer_111_4_3 p.15-17 Figure 3 notes; restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.12-13 Table 2 notes, Section 5.1.1), 1/5 `cluster_at_assignment_level` (ecta_2025_ecta22303 p.15-16 Section 3.2, a block bootstrap at the disaster level), 1/5 `cluster_at_unit_level`, 1/5 `few_clusters_addressed` and 1/5 `randomization_inference` (both restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.13-13 Section 5.1.1, Figure A4a), 1/5 `other:bootstrap_for_generated_regressor` (restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.19-20 Section 5.2.3, Table 5 notes), 1/5 `other:joint_test_over_bins` (restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.16-17 Section 5.1.3, Figure 2b notes), 4/5 `notes_state_se`, 2/5 `se_type_in_notes` and 1/5 `cluster_level_in_notes` (restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.25-25 Table 7 notes, where SE types differ by column). Across all methods, 57/91 notes record `no_justification_found`, 35/91 `cluster_at_assignment_level`, 24/91 `randomization_inference` and 12/91 `few_clusters_addressed` (`_all:aer_107_1_5 p.18-18 Section III.A`; `_all:aer_110_10_1 p.16-16 footnote 25`), and a permutation over placebo kink locations is recorded for a kink design (`_all:aer_108_4_3 p.22-23 Section IIIA, online Appendix B`). Practice diverges from the rule in two ways: two of the three notes with inference content give no rationale for the SE, and for aer_111_4_3 the paper states a bootstrap for the table that the traced call produces with the robust or default option (concordance se_type unknown 1), so the SE printed in the table may not be the one the card records. A joint test over contiguous bins and SUR across ranges (restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.14-15 Table 3 and Table 4 notes) are practices the rule does not mention.
- r6 (excess mass next to the elasticity, mapping and its assumptions, frictions, locality): 2/5 notes record `compared_to_literature` (aer_111_4_3 p.23-23 Section III.A), 2/5 `back_of_envelope`, 1/5 `relative_to_mean` and 1/5 `precision_discussed` (restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.12-13 Section 5.1.1 and 5.1.2, effects as shares of excess mass), 3/5 `identification_caveat` (ecta_2025_ecta22303 p.15-15 Section 3.2; aer_111_4_3 p.38-38 Section VII), 2/5 `scaling_discussed` and 2/5 `site_specific` (ecta_2025_ecta22303 p.37-37 Section 6, footnote 36). Practice diverges from the rule: no note records the mapping formula, a friction adjustment or the share of non-responders as such, and the literature benchmark is recorded without a statement about differences in kink size; the manipulation-design paper reports shares of excess mass as the rule's exception asks.

## Open questions for review

- Concordance between paper statements and recipe cards (`reported.md`, 5 notes, 60 items): agree 19, paper_only 33, unknown 6, code_only 1, disagree 1. paper_only is concentrated in robustness (5), data_source (4), outputs (4), controls (3), diagnostic (3) and sample (3). Implication: null card fields in those areas are recording gaps and must not be read as absence; `observed.md` cannot be cited as evidence that a diagnostic, placebo or robustness check was skipped. The estimator (agree 5; the paper_only 2 are structural components outside the bunching specifications) and weights (agree 3) fields are the card fields that can be trusted. The se_type unknown item (aer_111_4_3 s1, bootstrap in the paper against a robust option at the traced call) means the card's SE field is not the SE in the published table for that paper; r5's option-to-table trace is the check that would resolve it.
- Two of the three cards implement bunching as OLS on threshold-level or bin-level data and only one card records a bunching package call; no card records the counterfactual construction step (aer_111_4_3 d1 points at code/Codes/Bunching-estimation/02_bunching.do:1-11 without a specification record). A card revision that records the bin width, order and excluded window would let r2 be checked against code.
- restud_2026_restud_rdag071 is in this method's reported layer but its note records regression-discontinuity practice (RD plots, an optimal bandwidth per horizon, individual-level clustering) and its card is not in the bunching observed layer; the reviewer should decide whether it belongs here. It contributes to the denominators above but no rule cites it.
- r5 offers two bootstrap defaults (residuals of the bin-count fit; blocks at the threshold, cohort or shock level). Which one is the baseline in a given plan depends on whether the estimate is a single-threshold statistic or a cross-threshold comparison, and the plan author must state the choice; the reviewer should decide whether the document should name one default.
- r2 leaves open whether the missing-mass convergence rule for the upper bound of the excluded region should be the default for notches and whether the integration constraint should be required for kinks; both are described in the sources as choices, and the corpus records neither.
- Whether bunching used as a measure of manipulation or discretion (bin-indicator regressions, as in restud_2026_restud_rdag037) should stay inside this document as an exception to r1 and r6 or become its own branch with its own estimand statement.
- Whether r1 should require the statement that bunching identifies a local response for bunchers only, or whether that belongs with the writer role's limitation conventions; r6 repeats it as an interpretation requirement.
- No eval, template or skill exists for bunching; the validation column reads "no template yet" or "not testable by a template" throughout. A template would need at least a synthetic kink and a synthetic notch with a planted excess mass and a placebo threshold to test r2, r3 and r5.
- Dynamic bunching with repeated thresholds over time and bunching in two running variables are out of scope; no rule covers them.
