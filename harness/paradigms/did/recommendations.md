# Recommended rules: `did` (draft)

Written by the empirical-analyst role from the method literature in `harness/skills/did/references.md` and two layers of corpus evidence: observed code practice (the 17 validated cards listed in `observed.md`; 7 held out in harness/paradigms/did/reported.md; plus `../event_study/observed.md`, 10 cards) and reported paper practice (`reported.md`, generated from 32 paper-practice notes, 31 of them with a linked card, after the paper-level hold-out that removes 7 further notes). The observed layer records what the replication code does (code_trace); the reported layer records what the paper text says it does (author_statement, with page pointers). Observed and reported frequencies are evidence of practice only, not of correctness; there is no majority threshold. Rules are conditional; unknown card fields and unrecorded note tags are never counted as absence.

## Applicability

- Covered: two-period and multi-period DID, staggered adoption, event studies with leads and lags, triple differences, synthetic DID as a related estimator; panel or repeated cross-section with clustered assignment.
- Not covered: synthetic control with one treated unit, continuous-treatment DID (dose-response). Designs with treatment reversals are covered only by the classification and routing step in r3: no card in `observed.md` and no note in `reported.md` documents a reversible-treatment DID, so the guidance for that branch comes from the method literature alone and carries no corpus support in this set.

## Reported practice behind the rules (paper text)

Counts are papers among the 32 notes whose reader recorded the tag; an unrecorded tag means the reader did not record it, not that the paper lacks it. Pointers are `artid p.pages locator` as printed in `reported.md`; re-read the page before citing. This section records procedure (what was done), placement (where it is reported) and stated caveats only. It does not record any paper's findings, effect signs, magnitudes, or whether a test rejected, and it must not be read as endorsing or criticising any paper's inference.

- r1 (estimand and comparison group). 30/32 notes record `design_variant_named`, 20/32 `estimand_named`, 21/32 `identifying_assumption_explicit` and 22/32 `parallel_trends_argued`. Pointers: aer_111_12_8 p.1-3 Introduction, equations (1)-(3) (a new estimator introduced with its estimand written as equations), aer_111_12_2 p.1-4 Abstract and Introduction (matched DID with the estimand stated), ecta_2024_ecta19872 p.11-11 Section 5.2 Main Specification (parallel trends argued from the specification). Beyond the rule: the parallel-trends argument lives in the design section as a verbal argument about the comparison group, and in 5/32 notes part of the assumption statement is in a footnote (qje_140_1_10 p.23-23 Section IV.A.1, footnote 23 on continuous treatment intensity). Contradiction with the cards: the comparison group is described in the text but the card `comparison_group` field is null in 16 concordance items (paper_only) against 2 that agree; the reported layer, not the observed layer, is currently the only source for this field.
- r2 (heterogeneity-robust estimators under staggered timing). Staggered-adoption estimators are named in the text in a minority of notes: 1/32 `other:heterogeneous_treatment_effect_estimator` (aer_113_1_1 p.13-17 Table 3 column 5/10; Table 4 discussion), 1/32 `other:staggered_did_bias_check` (aer_114_11_7 p.28-29 Section III.D), 1/32 `other:goodman_bacon_decomposition` (aer_114_7_10 p.32-32 Section VI). ## Open questions for review

- Unresolved, `needed_from: literature`: branch (iv) of r4 (spatial dependence) has no method source in `harness/skills/did/references.md`. The corpus records the practice (`spatial_hac` 2/32, `other:driscoll_kraay` 1/32) but a citable source for the cutoff and kernel choice is `unverified` here and must be resolved before r4 leaves draft.
- Unresolved, `needed_from: literature`: whether an online appendix counts as part of the published record is not established by any pointer in `reported.md` or by any source listed here. r11 was rewritten to avoid depending on that boundary (it now requires a main-text statement of the comparison and its location); if the boundary is later established, r11 can be tightened again.
- Unresolved, `needed_from: user`: whether the reversal branch of r3 should block a design (no reversible-treatment DID may be run with an absorbing-path estimator) or only require a stated justification. No card and no note in this set bears on the choice.
- Whether to require a heterogeneity-robust estimator as the main specification or accept TWFE with a decomposition when weights are shown to be positive.
- Default handling of always-treated units and of the last cohort without not-yet-treated comparisons.
- Whether the event-study cards recorded under `event_study` (PPML-heavy, 10 cards) should be merged into this paradigm; the generator supports `--also event_study`.
- Concordance between paper statements and cards (reported.md, 32 notes, 31 with a card): 380 compared items, of which 181 agree, 155 are paper_only, 13 disagree, 8 are code_only and 23 are unknown. Papers in the paper-level hold-out (harness/paradigms/did/reported.md, 7 notes) are excluded from this method's reported layer and count only in `harness/paradigms/_all/reported.md`. The paper_only items concentrate in comparison_group (16 paper_only against 2 agree), robustness (19 against 9), data_source (17 against 12), outputs (15 against 4), fixed_effects (15 against 21), diagnostic (14 against 20) and sample (14 against 10). Implication: a null card field in these categories is an omission of the card extraction, not evidence that the paper lacks the item, and the reported layer should be consulted before a rule cites a card as evidence of absence.
