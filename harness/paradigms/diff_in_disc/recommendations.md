# Recommended rules: `diff_in_disc` (draft)

Written by the empirical-analyst role from the difference-in-discontinuities method literature and from one generated layer of corpus practice. There is no observed layer for this method: no accepted recipe card is tagged `diff_in_disc`, so `observed.md` does not exist and no rule cites a code observation. The reported layer (`reported.md`) holds 3 paper-practice notes, 2 of which have a linked recipe card (jpe_132_9_2, restud_2026_restud_rdag037); the card for jpe_132_9_2 enters only through the concordance table, because that note records no tag under the diff-in-disc categories. This evidence base is thin: counts of the form k/3 cannot show convention, and the two designs the notes describe (a spatial RD with a before-after contrast, and an RD in time with a before-after contrast) do not include the canonical population-threshold case that the method literature was written for. Reported frequencies are evidence of practice only, never of correctness; a tag absent from a note means "not recorded". The design is an extension of RD, so rules below refer to the rdd rules in `harness/paradigms/rdd/recommendations.md` by id (rdd r1 to r12) instead of repeating them. The document stays `draft` until its rules have been through an independent check and the open questions below are settled; check records live in `harness_build/pilot/runs/`.

## Applicability

- Covered: sharp designs in which one RD cutoff (a population threshold, an eligibility score, a geographic boundary reduced to signed distance, or a date) is combined with a second contrast at the same cutoff: before versus after a reform, or units subject to the cutoff-triggered treatment versus units facing the same cutoff without it. The estimand is the difference between the two RD jumps. Cross-section, repeated cross-section and panel data.
- Not covered or a different branch: plain RD without a second contrast (rdd rules); plain difference-in-differences or event studies without a cutoff (did rules); fuzzy difference-in-discontinuities (a first stage in each contrast; no rule yet, see open questions); bunching estimands; two-dimensional boundary designs that are not reduced to a distance running variable; dynamic RD with repeated crossings.

| r1 | State the estimand as the difference between two RD jumps at the same cutoff: the jump in the treated contrast (post period or treated group) minus the jump in the comparison contrast (pre period or untreated group), local to units at the cutoff (rdd r11). State the two assumptions the design adds to continuity at the cutoff within each contrast (rdd r1): (a) the discontinuity measured in the comparison contrast would have carried over unchanged to the treated contrast without the treatment, that is a parallel-trends condition restricted to the cutoff; (b) the treatment effect at the cutoff does not interact with the confounding discontinuity. Name which contrast supplies the second difference (time or group) and why plain RD fails at this cutoff (another policy sharing the threshold, a documented pre-existing jump, or a boundary that changes several things at once). | the cutoff carries more than the treatment of interest, or a pre-existing jump is documented, and a before-after or treated-control contrast exists at the same cutoff | when no comparison-contrast jump is present and no compound treatment is plausible, keep plain RD as the main specification (rdd r1) and report the difference as a variant; for RD in time with one series there is no cross-sectional contrast and assumption (a) is about other events at the same date | Grembi, Nannicini, Troiano 2016 (verified_online; the assumption wording is paraphrased from their identification section and the page mapping was not re-read: unverified); Eggers, Freier, Grembi, Nannicini 2018 (verified_online); Lalive 2008 (verified_online; early application); Hausman, Rapson 2018 (verified_online); Butts, Difference-in-Discontinuities, working paper (unverified) | jpe_2026_739821 p.29-30 Section V.C, equations (4)-(5); restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.22-23 Section 5.3, equation (6) | not testable by a template (statement rule) |
| r2 | Estimate and report the RD jump in the comparison contrast alone next to the difference, and run the rdd r3 checks (density test at the cutoff, covariate balance at the cutoff) separately within each contrast; add the covariate analogue of assumption (a): the jump in each predetermined covariate must not change between contrasts, reported as a table with one column per contrast and a difference column. When more than two periods exist, plot the outcome jump by period and show it is stable before treatment. Decision rule: a nonzero comparison-contrast jump in the outcome is the motivation for the design, not a failure; a change across contrasts in a covariate jump or in the density jump is a threat and is stated next to the main estimate. | always | RD in time with one series has no second contrast for a density test; check instead for anticipation or bunching of observations around the date and state the check | Grembi, Nannicini, Troiano 2016 (verified_online); Cattaneo, Jansson, Ma 2020 (verified_online); Lee, Lemieux 2010 (verified_online); Hausman, Rapson 2018 (verified_online); Roth 2022 (verified_online; the period plot is a pre-test and selecting on it is the caveat) | restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.22-23 Section 5.3, Figure A11; jpe_2026_739821 p.31-32 Figure 5; _all: aer_111_12_2 p.21-22 Figure 3 and Figure 4, panel B (period-by-period plot in a DID setting, not at a cutoff) | no template yet |
| r3 | Use a local polynomial of order 1 in the running variable on each side and within each contrast (rdd r1), with one common bandwidth for both contrasts so the two jumps are comparable. Choose it with an MSE-optimal selector run on the pooled sample or, when run per contrast, take the narrower one and state the rule. Report the bandwidth sensitivity grid and a polynomial-order variant (rdd r4) for the difference itself, not only for each jump. State what the running variable is (population count, score, signed distance to a boundary, or time), how it was constructed, and its frequency when it is time. | always | discrete running variables with few support points (integer counts at a population threshold are usually fine; time at a coarse frequency is not): apply rdd r6 to each jump and say the honest CI does not extend to the difference in this harness; bandwidths fixed by the institutional setting (a fixed distance band) are stated as such and varied in the grid | Calonico, Cattaneo, Titiunik 2014 (verified_online); Imbens, Kalyanaraman 2012 (verified_online); Gelman, Imbens 2019 (verified_online); Cattaneo, Titiunik 2022 (verified_online); Grembi, Nannicini, Troiano 2016 (verified_online; common-bandwidth practice); Keele, Titiunik 2015 (verified_online; distance running variable); Hausman, Rapson 2018 (verified_online; time running variable) | jpe_2026_739821 p.34-34 Section V.C, robustness discussion (Figure E3, appendix); restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.23-24 Table 6, Figure A11, Section 5.3; jpe_2026_739821 p.30-30 Section V.C, equation (4); concordance row bandwidth agree: jpe_132_9_2 x2 | no template yet (the rdd template grid runs per contrast but does not compute the difference) |
| r4 | Estimate the main specification as one local regression on the pooled sample inside the bandwidth: outcome on the side indicator, the centred running variable, their interaction, the contrast indicator (post or treated group), and the contrast interacted with each of the three; the coefficient on side times contrast is the estimate. Report per column: that coefficient with its SE or CI, the jump within each contrast, the effective N in each of the four cells (side times contrast), the bandwidth, and the outcome mean in the comparison cell on the untreated side. Table notes state polynomial order, kernel, bandwidth selector, SE type and cluster level, and the fixed-effect and covariate set (rdd r2, rdd r9). Map every column to a plan specification id. | always | with many periods or many groups the pooled regression carries period or group fixed effects and the contrast becomes an event-time or group-specific interaction; state the aggregation, and when adoption is staggered apply the did rules on staggered timing to the second difference | Grembi, Nannicini, Troiano 2016 (verified_online; estimating equation form); Calonico, Cattaneo, Farrell, Titiunik 2017 rdrobust (verified_online; reporting fields) | restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.23-23 Table 6 and notes; jpe_2026_739821 p.33-33 Table 4; jpe_2026_739821 p.23-33 Table 2 note; Table 4 note | no template yet |
| r5 | Cluster at the level at which the cutoff-defining unit is assigned (the municipality when the running variable is municipal population; the geographic unit or boundary segment when the cutoff is a border), never finer, and pass the same cluster variable to the per-contrast RD calls and to the pooled regression (rdd r5). Report the number of clusters on each side of the cutoff inside the bandwidth and within each contrast, and plan a wild cluster bootstrap or randomization inference when either count is small. With a distance running variable add Conley spatial standard errors under stated distance cutoffs as a variant; with a time running variable use HAC with a stated lag rule or cluster by the time unit, and state which. State the choice and its reason in the table notes for the RD columns and the difference columns alike. | always | an independent cross-section with one observation per cutoff-defining unit and no repeat: heteroskedasticity-robust nearest-neighbour variance, stated as such | Abadie, Athey, Imbens, Wooldridge 2022 (verified_online); MacKinnon, Nielsen, Webb 2023 (verified_online); Bertrand, Duflo, Mullainathan 2004 (verified_online); Cameron, Gelbach, Miller 2008 (verified_online); Conley 1999 (verified_online); Hausman, Rapson 2018 (verified_online); Keele, Titiunik 2015 (verified_online) | jpe_2026_739821 p.16-34 Section IV.A; Section V.B 'Spatially adjusted errors'; Section V.C robustness; jpe_2026_739821 p.23-33 Table 2 note; Table 4 note; concordance row cluster_level paper_only: restud_2026_restud_rdag037 s1.se.cluster_level; _all: aer_110_2_5 p.14-14 Section II.B, footnote 23; _all: aer_110_10_1 p.16-16 footnote 25 | no template yet |
| r6 | Beyond the rdd r4 set (bandwidth grid, polynomial variant, placebo cutoffs with treatment held fixed), report placebos aimed at the second difference: a placebo contrast at the true cutoff (two pre-periods, or two untreated groups) that must show no change in the jump; placebo dates at nearby non-treatment events for RD in time; and a donut variant dropping observations nearest the cutoff when heaping in time or space is visible. Collect these in a robustness subsection with full tables in the appendix and cite each from the main text (rdd r10). State in the limitations that the estimate is local to the cutoff and to the contrast used, and that the design cannot separate the treatment from any other change that occurred at the same cutoff in the same contrast. | always | with a single pre-period the placebo contrast is not available; say so and rely on the covariate-jump stability check in r2 | Grembi, Nannicini, Troiano 2016 (verified_online); Eggers, Freier, Grembi, Nannicini 2018 (verified_online); Hausman, Rapson 2018 (verified_online); Cattaneo, Titiunik 2022 (verified_online); placement and limitation wording: corpus practice only | restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.23-24 footnote 18, Table A16; jpe_2026_739821 p.34-34 Section V.C, robustness discussion (Figure E3, appendix); _all: aer_107_1_5 p.22-23 Figure 7 | not testable by a template (placement and limitation parts); placebo contrast: no template yet |

## Reported practice behind the rules (paper text)

Counts are papers out of the 3 notes in `reported.md`; a note that lacks a tag did not record it. Cross-method counts are out of the 91 notes in `harness/paradigms/_all/reported.md` and carry the `_all:` prefix. Pointers are artid, page range and locator as printed; re-read the page before citing.

- r1 (estimand and the two added assumptions): 2/3 notes record `design_variant_named` and 2/3 `continuity_at_cutoff_argued` (jpe_2026_739821 p.29-30 Section V.C, equations (4)-(5); restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.22-23 Section 5.3, equation (6)). Practice diverges from the rule in what is recorded: the reader tags do not separate RD continuity from the added stability assumption (a), so whether either paper states (a) or (b) explicitly is not recorded, and the rule's assumption list has no reported basis beyond the fact that a second design was named. The two variants named are a spatial RD with a before-after contrast and an RD in time with a before-after contrast; the population-threshold case with a compound treatment has no note.
- r2 (comparison-contrast jump and per-contrast checks): 1/3 notes record `density_test` (restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.22-23 Section 5.3, Figure A11), 2/3 `in_appendix`, 1/3 `in_main_text`; no note records `balance_table` in this method's categories, and 1/3 records `rd_plot` and `binscatter` (jpe_2026_739821 p.31-32 Figure 5). The one recorded check is a stability-over-years check of a pre-reform share, which is the period-by-period form of the rule rather than a density test proper; the covariate-jump stability table the rule asks for is not recorded in any note. In `_all`, 36/91 notes record `pre_trends_plot` (aer_111_12_2 p.21-22 Figure 3 and Figure 4, panel B), so the period plot is common practice in DID settings but has no recorded instance at a cutoff.
- r3 (order 1, common bandwidth, grid for the difference): 2/3 notes record `bandwidth_sensitivity` (jpe_2026_739821 p.34-34 Section V.C, robustness discussion (Figure E3, appendix); restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.23-24 Table 6, Figure A11, Section 5.3) and 1/3 `running_variable_definition` (jpe_2026_739821 p.30-30 Section V.C, equation (4)). The concordance table has one bandwidth row, agree (jpe_132_9_2 x2), where the paper and the card both record an MSE-optimal selector. Whether one bandwidth is shared across contrasts, and whether the grid is reported for the difference or for each jump, is not recorded by any note; the rule's common-bandwidth requirement therefore rests on the method literature alone.
- r4 (pooled specification and table content): 2/3 notes record `se_type_in_notes` (jpe_2026_739821 p.23-33 Table 2 note; Table 4 note; restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.23-23 Table 6 and notes), 1/3 `cluster_level_in_notes`, 1/3 `mean_of_dependent_reported` (jpe_2026_739821 p.33-33 Table 4) and 1/3 `controls_progressive_columns`. Practice goes beyond the rule in one respect: the pooled discontinuity and the per-period discontinuities are shown as separate columns of one table (restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.23-23 Table 6 and notes), which the rule only asks for as a row. No note records effective N per cell, and the concordance shows fixed_effects as paper_only in 3 rows, so the cards cannot be used to check what the baseline column carries.
- r5 (cluster at the cutoff-defining unit, count clusters, spatial or HAC variants): 1/3 notes record `spatial_hac` (jpe_2026_739821 p.16-34 Section IV.A; Section V.B 'Spatially adjusted errors'; Section V.C robustness) and 1/3 `cluster_level_in_notes` (jpe_2026_739821 p.23-33 Table 2 note; Table 4 note); the third note records no inference justification. In `_all`, 35/91 record `cluster_at_assignment_level`, 10/91 `spatial_hac` (aer_110_2_5 p.14-14 Section II.B, footnote 23) and 12/91 `few_clusters_addressed` (aer_110_10_1 p.16-16 footnote 25). Practice diverges from the rule in both directions: two reader gaps state that unclustered RD standard errors are reported next to clustered DD standard errors without a stated reason, and no note records the number of clusters inside the bandwidth or a few-cluster plan; the concordance row cluster_level is paper_only in 2 cases (restud_2026_restud_rdag037 s1.se.cluster_level among them), so the cards did not record the level the papers state.
- r6 (placebo contrast, placebo dates, donut, placement, limitation): 1/3 notes record `placebo_cutoffs` in the form of placebo dates (restud_2026_restud_rdag037 p.23-24 footnote 18, Table A16) and 2/3 `bandwidth_sensitivity` placed `in_appendix` (jpe_2026_739821 p.34-34 Section V.C, robustness discussion (Figure E3, appendix)). In `_all`, 15/91 record `placebo_cutoffs` (aer_107_1_5 p.22-23 Figure 7). No note records a placebo contrast built from two untreated periods or groups, or a donut variant, and the one `limitation_stated` tag (1/3) concerns data coverage rather than the design; the limitation sentence in the rule has no reported basis in this method's notes.

## Open questions for review

- There is no observed layer. The two notes with a card (jpe_132_9_2, restud_2026_restud_rdag037) carry cards distilled under other methods; the reviewer should decide whether `fh distill diff_in_disc` should be run on those cards once they are tagged, or whether the method waits for new cards. Until then no rule can cite a code observation, and the validation column has no template.
- jpe_132_9_2 is a held-out paper for the rdd and iv skill evaluations (`harness_build/evals/holdout/rdd.json`, `harness_build/evals/holdout/iv.json`) but there is no hold-out list for diff_in_disc, so its note is included here and its concordance rows are cited in r3 and r5. The reviewer should decide whether the paper-level hold-out extends to methods that share the paper.
- Bandwidth selection for the difference (r3): no source in the list provides an MSE-optimal selector for the interaction coefficient; the pooled-sample or narrower-per-contrast rule is a harness convention and needs a decision or a source.
- The assumption wording in r1 is paraphrased from the identification section of Grembi, Nannicini and Troiano (2016) without re-reading the page in this pass; the Butts working paper on the same assumptions is cited from memory and is unverified. Both need a page-level check before the rule leaves draft.
- Fuzzy difference-in-discontinuities (a first stage within each contrast) has no rule; the reviewer should decide whether it is a branch of r4 or a separate document.
- RD in time (restud_2026_restud_rdag037) is treated here as a diff-in-disc case because the note names a before-after contrast at the date; the reviewer should decide whether it belongs to this method or to a separate `rd_in_time` branch, since Hausman and Rapson (2018) treat it as a distinct design with its own inference problems.
- Concordance between paper statements and recipe cards (`reported.md`, 3 notes, 22 items): agree 8, paper_only 12, unknown 2, disagree 0, code_only 0. paper_only is concentrated in fixed_effects (3), cluster_level (2), diagnostic (2), outputs (2) and one row each of data_source, robustness and sample. Implication: card fields for fixed effects, cluster level, diagnostics and outputs are recording gaps for these two papers and must not be read as absence; the estimator (agree 2) and se_type (agree 2) fields are the ones that can be trusted, consistent with the rdd finding.
- r4 (table content) and the placement and limitation parts of r6 are reporting rules taken from paper practice and the rdd exemplar; they need a decision on whether reporting rules belong here or in the writer role's conventions.
