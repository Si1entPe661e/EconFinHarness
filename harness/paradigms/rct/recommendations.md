# Recommended rules: `rct` (draft)

Written by the empirical-analyst role from the method literature listed under "Method sources" and from two generated layers of corpus practice: observed code practice in `observed.md` (7 validated recipe cards, 6 of them with partial coverage, 29 specifications; the two held-out papers aer_114_12_7 and ecta_2026_ecta21180 are excluded) and reported paper practice in `reported.md` (13 paper-practice notes taken from the paper text, 11 of which have a linked recipe card; the same hold-out applies). Only 7 of the 11 linked cards are in the observed distillation set, so four cards (ecta_92_2_2, ecta_2025_ecta19303, jpe_133_5_2, rfs_2026_rfs_hhaf079) are visible here only through the concordance table of `reported.md`. The evidence base is thin: no design variant is recorded on any card, the diagnostics facet is `not_found_in_scope` or `unknown` for most cards, 6/7 cards have no specification-to-output links, and one of the seven cards (aer_107_5_108) is a machine-learning heterogeneity method paper whose experimental part is small. Observed and reported frequencies are evidence of practice only, not of correctness; there is no majority threshold, and a tag absent from a note or a null card field means "not recorded", not "not done". Every rule states when it applies, its exceptions, its sources with verification status, corpus pointers (artid + record id + path:lines from `observed.md`; artid + page + locator from `reported.md`) and the validation result. The document stays `draft` until its rules have been through an independent check and the open questions below are settled; check records live in `harness_build/pilot/runs/`.

## Applicability

- Covered: field experiments and survey or information-provision experiments in which the researcher (or a partner following the researcher's protocol) randomizes assignment at the individual, household, session, branch, school, village or market level; simple, stratified, blocked or paired randomization; multi-arm designs with a pure or active control; encouragement designs with imperfect take-up; two-stage (saturation) designs for spillovers; outcomes from a single endline, a baseline-endline pair, linked administrative records, or a short follow-up panel.
- Not covered or a different branch: lotteries and rollouts administered without a researcher protocol (they share r1, r6 and r7 only; the rest is the relevant quasi-experimental paradigm); within-subject laboratory designs; adaptive or bandit assignment; machine-learning heterogeneity estimation on experimental data (causal forests and honest splitting, present in aer_107_5_108, are a separate branch); structural estimation that uses experimental variation as moments; regression adjustment with nonlinear outcome models beyond the linear and post-double-selection cases named in r2.

## Open questions for review

- Concordance between paper statements and recipe cards (`reported.md`, 11 notes with a card, 136 items): agree 67, paper_only 56, unknown 8, disagree 4, code_only 1. paper_only is concentrated in diagnostic (9), comparison_group (7), sample (7), controls (6), robustness (6), outputs (5), fixed_effects (5) and cluster_level (4). Implication: a null card field or an `unknown` or `not_found_in_scope` diagnostic is a recording gap, and observed.md counts for balance, attrition, first stage, multiple testing and robustness must not be cited as evidence that a check was skipped. The estimator field (agree 10, paper_only 1, disagree 0) and se_type (agree 7, paper_only 1, disagree 1) are the card fields that can be trusted most; cluster_level (agree 5, paper_only 4, unknown 2, disagree 1) needs a trace to the call before use.
- The four disagreements: aer_107_5_108 s1 cluster level (paper: individual; card: a group variable), rfs_2026_rfs_hhaf079 s1 and s2 SE type (paper: robust with Anderson-Rubin intervals; card: cluster with a null level), and the output counts for jpe_2025_736209 and jpe_2025_736212 (cards record fewer or no tables). Implication: r6 pointers to card SE fields are provisional until the cluster variable is resolved.
- Only 7 of the 11 linked cards are in the observed distillation (ecta_92_2_2, ecta_2025_ecta19303, jpe_133_5_2 and rfs_2026_rfs_hhaf079 are absent). The reviewer should confirm whether these cards carry a different primary method tag or failed validation, and whether the rct observed layer should be regenerated to include them; ecta_92_2_2 in particular is the only card with a traced randomization-inference call.
- r2: whether the headline column should be the unadjusted or the prespecified-adjusted estimate, and whether the fully interacted adjustment of Lin 2013 should be recommended over the plain additive adjustment; the corpus shows both plain covariates and post-double-selection lasso and gives no basis to choose.
- r6: how to handle two-level designs (individual assignment within sessions) where one corpus paper reports robust headline SEs with session-clustered bootstrap p-values; the rule's "unit of randomization" wording does not settle this. Also whether a numeric few-cluster guideline should be written; the rule deliberately has none.
