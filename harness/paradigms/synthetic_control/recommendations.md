# Recommended rules: `synthetic_control` (draft)

Written by the empirical-analyst role from the synthetic control and synthetic difference-in-differences literature listed under "Method sources" and from two generated layers of corpus practice: observed code practice in `observed.md` (2 validated recipe cards, 2 designs, 5 specifications; one card is minimal coverage and one partial) and reported paper practice in `reported.md` (6 paper-practice notes taken from the paper text, all 6 with a linked recipe card; the two cards in `observed.md` are among these 6). The evidence base is thin: two of the six notes are methods papers rather than applications, the observed layer records no design variant and no specification-to-output link, and no note comes from a paper whose headline design is a classic single-treated-unit synthetic control with a covariate predictor set. Observed frequencies and reported frequencies are evidence of practice only, never of correctness; there is no majority threshold, and a tag absent from a note or a null card field means "not recorded", not "not done". Every rule states when it applies, its exceptions, its sources with verification status, corpus pointers (artid + record id from `observed.md`; artid + page + locator from `reported.md`; `_all:` prefix for `harness/paradigms/_all/reported.md`) and the validation result. The document stays `draft` until its rules have been through an independent check and the open questions below are settled; historical build and review records are not distributed.

## Applicability

- Covered: comparative case studies with one or a few treated aggregate units (regions, countries, firms, markets) observed over a long pre-treatment window and a donor pool of untreated units; the classic synthetic control estimator with unit weights, its penalised and augmented variants, and synthetic difference-in-differences (unit and time weights) with one or several treated units and possibly staggered adoption.
- Not covered or a different branch: two-way fixed-effects and heterogeneity-robust difference-in-differences with many treated units (`harness/paradigms/did/`), event studies with individual-level treatment (`event_study`), matrix-completion and factor-model imputation estimators (mentioned only as comparison columns), and time-series interrupted designs with no donor pool.

## Open questions for review

- Evidence base: 2 cards (one minimal, one partial coverage; no design variant recorded; no specification-to-output links) and 6 notes, of which 2 are methods papers (aer_111_12_8, jpe_2026_742424) and 4 are applications where synthetic DID is a secondary or comparison design. No note or card in this set is a classic single-treated-unit synthetic control application with a covariate predictor set; the reviewer should decide whether rules r1, r2 and r4 can leave draft without at least one such card.
- Concordance between paper statements and recipe cards (`reported.md`, 6 notes, 72 items): agree 38, paper_only 26, unknown 4, disagree 3, code_only 1. paper_only is concentrated in robustness (4), controls (3), data_source (3), diagnostic (3), estimator (3), comparison_group (2), fixed_effects (2), outputs (2) and sample (2). Implication: the observed layer's empty diagnostic and robustness facets are recording gaps, not evidence that placebo runs or donor exclusions were skipped; the estimator field (agree 10, disagree 0) and se_type (agree 5, disagree 1) are the card fields that can be trusted most. The one se_type disagreement (jpe_2026_742424 s1, card says cluster, paper says a self-normalised statistic) and the one cluster_level disagreement (restud_2026_restud_rdaf071 s2, district in code versus village in the table notes) should be re-read before either card is cited for inference practice.
- r3 lists several inference options (permutation, conformal, prediction intervals, synthetic DID variance estimators) without a default. A reviewer or the user should decide whether the harness prescribes one default per setting (single treated unit with many donors; few treated units; many treated units) or keeps the menu with a required justification sentence.
- r3 asks for a rule for dropping poorly fitting placebos in the permutation distribution; the literature uses several conventions (RMSPE multiples) and none is adopted here. A numeric convention, if any, is a harness choice, not a literature value.
- r4 leave-one-out and r5 donor counts per treated unit have no reported basis in the 6 notes; the reviewer should confirm that the method literature alone is sufficient support, or downgrade them to optional checks.
- r2 requires the pre-period RMSPE in table or figure notes; no note records it. Whether a plot alone satisfies the rule is a reviewer decision.
- The synthetic DID Stata command documentation could not be resolved through Crossref in this pass (unverified); the R `synthdid` package is not on CRAN and carries no DOI. Software behaviour claims in r3 (replication counts, seeds, variance-estimator names) must be checked against the installed versions before a template is written.
- Rules r2, r4 (placement half) and r6 are reporting and interpretation rules; they need a reviewer decision on whether they belong in this document or in the writer role's conventions, as for the rdd draft.
- No synthetic control template evaluation was available when this draft was written, so its validation cells read `no template yet` or `not testable by a template`.
