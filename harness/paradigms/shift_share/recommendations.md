# Recommended rules: `shift_share` (draft)

Written by the empirical-analyst role from the shift-share method literature (sources listed at the end, each with a verification status) and two generated evidence layers: observed code practice in `observed.md` (3 validated recipe cards, all `partial` coverage, 3 designs and 8 specifications, no design variant recorded) and reported paper practice in `reported.md` (8 paper-practice notes, all 8 with a linked recipe card, of which 6 are full-text reads, 1 main-text and 1 partial). The evidence base is thin: `observed.md` itself states that shares over fewer than 5 papers are not meaningful and that variant-conditional rules cannot be derived from it, and the 8 notes include papers where shift-share is a secondary design (an IV step inside a diffusion or difference-in-differences paper). One of the 8 notes (restud_89_1_6) is the method paper whose replication archive implements the shock-level framework, so several of the pointers below come from a paper that recommends the practice rather than merely uses it. Observed and reported frequencies are evidence of practice, not of correctness; there is no majority threshold; a null card field or a tag absent from a note means "not recorded", never "not done". Rules marked with an `iv` id reuse the corresponding rule in `harness/paradigms/iv/recommendations.md` and only add what is specific to the shift-share structure.

## Applicability

- Covered: linear shift-share (Bartik) instruments and shift-share exposure designs where an outcome at the unit level (region, firm, county, municipality) is regressed on an exposure measure built as a share-weighted sum of shocks (or on its instrumented counterpart); cross-section, repeated cross-section and panel; share exogeneity (Goldsmith-Pinkham, Sorkin, Swift) and shock exogeneity (Borusyak, Hull, Jaravel; Adao, Kolesar, Morales) branches; one or several shift-share instruments; exposure designs with a single shift (a "treatment intensity times post" interaction) for the identification-statement, inference and denominator rules only.
- Not covered or a different branch: nonlinear exposure models, network and spatial spillover instruments beyond the share structure, shift-share designs with endogenous shares that are themselves instrumented, structural gravity-based instruments, and the weak-instrument and complier reporting rules that live in `harness/paradigms/iv/recommendations.md` (r3, r8, r9, r11). Staggered exposure difference-in-differences with heterogeneous timing belongs to `did`.

## Reported practice behind the rules (paper text)

Counts are papers out of the 8 notes in `reported.md`; a note that lacks a tag did not record it. Pointers are artid, page range and locator as printed in `reported.md`; re-read the page before citing. No paper-level hold-out applies to this method (`reported.md` lists no excluded notes).

- r1 (branch and identification statement): 5/8 notes record `identifying_assumption_explicit`, 6/8 `instrument_exclusion_argued`, 5/8 `estimand_named` and 7/8 `design_variant_named` (aer_112_2_1 p.11-17 Section III, Empirical Strategy; aer_113_3_7 p.24-24 Section III.B; rfs_2026_rfs_hhaf080 p.37-39 Section 4.3.1, footnote 39). One note writes the condition as a formal orthogonality restriction (aer_112_2_1 p.15-15 Section III, Identifying Assumption and Validity Checks, equation 7) and one argues share exogeneity from pre-exposure shares (qje_140_1_10 p.32-33 Section V.A.1). Divergence from the rule: the reader paraphrases do not record an explicit choice between the share and the shock branch in any applied note; only the method paper (restud_89_1_6 p.5-6 Section 2.1, equations (1)-(3)) sets the branch out formally, and the cards record no design variant, so the branch is unobservable in code.
- r2 (share-branch diagnostics): 0/8 notes record Rotemberg weights, a per-component first stage or a share-level balance regression on high-weight components. What the notes do record is a pre-period placebo table (`pre_trends_test` 2/8: aer_112_2_1 p.16-16 Table 1), a unit-level balance table (`balance_table` 2/8: qje_140_1_10 p.34-34 Table V), a footnote on further baseline characteristics that the instrument does not predict (aer_112_2_1 p.16-16 Section III, footnote 13) and a lagged-shares rebuild (rfs_2026_rfs_hhaf080 p.42-42 Section 4.3.3, Internet Appendix Table I.1). Practice diverges from the rule: the share-branch checks in the corpus are unit-level, not component-level, and no reader gap or note mentions the weights; the rule rests on the method source alone.
- r3 (shock-branch diagnostics): 1/8 notes record `other:effective_sample_size_reported` and 1/8 `other:shock_distribution_summary`, both in the method paper (restud_89_1_6 p.24-24 Section 6.2.2, Table 1; restud_89_1_6 p.23-25 Section 6.2.2, Table 1), which also reports shock-level balance and pre-trends in one table (restud_89_1_6 p.26-27 Section 6.2.3, Table 3 Panels A and B). One applied note points to appendix tables implementing the recent shift-share diagnostics without the reader naming them (qje_140_1_10 p.55-55 Section VII.A). Divergence: outside the method paper no note records an effective number of shocks, and two reader gaps state that no balance table for the instrument or no few-cluster discussion is given, so the rule is ahead of the applied practice seen here.
- r4 (share denominator): 1/8 notes record `other:incomplete_shares_control` (restud_89_1_6 p.14-16 Section 4.2, Section 4.3, footnote 31). 6/8 record `instrument_construction`, and those passages state the base period of the shares (aer_112_2_1 p.13-14 Section III, equations 2-3; qje_140_1_10 p.31-31 Section V.A.1, equation (5); restud_2026_restud_rdag027 p.16-17 Section 4.2, equations (4.2) and (4.3)); one note separates shares, shocks and instrument in notation (restud_89_1_6 p.21-22 Section 6.2.1, footnote 39). ## Open questions for review

- Whether the harness should require the branch choice in r1 as a plan field (`design.variant` = `shares_exogenous` or `shocks_exogenous`) so that cards can record it; at present the variant is unknown in 3/3 cards and the branch has to be read from the paper text.
- Whether r2 should require Rotemberg weights in the main text or accept an appendix table with a pointer; no corpus note reports them at all, so there is no observed placement convention.
- Implementation equivalence of shock-level inference across languages: the Stata `ssaggregate` route (regression on aggregated shock-level data with clustered SEs) and the R `ShiftShareSE` route (exposure-robust variance on the unit-level regression) target the same variance under different formulas and controls handling; a synthetic test is needed before r5 can name a default implementation, and the harness has no shift-share template yet.
- Whether few effective shocks should trigger the null-imposed confidence set (as implemented in restud_89_1_6 s4) or randomisation over shocks (as in aer_112_2_1) as the default; the two answer different questions and the rule currently lists both.
- Whether the lagged-instrument variant in r6 (Jaeger, Ruist, Stuhler 2018, unverified) belongs in a recommendation with an unverified source or should be moved to a remark until the reference is verified.
- Whether the recentering half of r4 (Borusyak, Hull 2023) is within scope for this method or belongs to a separate `exposure_design` branch; it has no corpus support in either layer.
- Concordance between paper statements and cards (`reported.md`, 8 notes all with a linked card, 102 compared items): agree 51, paper_only 43, code_only 3, disagree 2, unknown 3. paper_only concentrates in outputs (7), data_source (6), diagnostic (6), controls (5), robustness (5), sample (5) and comparison_group (4), whereas estimator (agree 10, code_only 1), fixed_effects (agree 10, paper_only 2, disagree 1), cluster_level (agree 11, paper_only 1, code_only 1, unknown 1) and weights (agree 6) mostly agree. Implication: card fields for estimator, cluster level and weights can be used for counting in this method; null card fields for diagnostics, robustness, controls, sample and comparison group are recording gaps and must not be cited as evidence that a check (Rotemberg weights, shock-level balance, leave-one-out) was skipped. The two disagreements are one fixed-effects set (qje_140_1_4 s1) and one output count (aer_112_2_1), so a card specification may be a variant rather than the paper's baseline; r7 pointers to table content therefore come from the paper layer only.
- All 3 cards are `partial` coverage and the shock-level code observations come from one method archive (restud_89_1_6); a reviewer should decide whether rules r3 and r5 may cite a method paper's own application as corpus practice, or whether those pointers should be labelled as method-paper illustrations.
- Rules r7 and r8 are reporting and interpretation rules; they need a reviewer decision on whether they belong here or in the writer role's conventions, and they cannot be tested by a template.
