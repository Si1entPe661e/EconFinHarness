# Design an empirical study

Select the relevant main method through the [composition guide](../skill-composition.md). Combine the `empirical-analyst` responsibility with `panel-data-checks` and `inference-clustering`; use `literature-verification` for source support.

- Define the question, estimand, comparison or prediction target and available material. State identification or model assumptions.
- Choose observation units, sample, variable timing, weights, controls and effects. Prediction tasks define information availability and evaluation splits.
- Choose inference from assignment, sampling and dependence. Consider effective observations, clusters, support and specialized uncertainty.
- Specify main results, key diagnostics and necessary sensitivities for `tables-figures`. Findings from the [analysis workflow](empirical-analysis.md) may require revisiting the design.

Deliver a practical design. Ask about consequential missing inputs while continuing unaffected work. Read only relevant skills.
