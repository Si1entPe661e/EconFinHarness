# Prepare a briefing or decision memo

Use `briefing-writing` to identify what the audience must know or decide. Establish actual status with `project-status` when relevant. Select evidence, preserve essential qualifications and state recommendations, viable alternatives and next actions.

For slides, build a narrative with one purpose per slide and use the available presentation skill for the actual artifact. For a short written update, keep the medium simple. Deliver the briefing, its material evidence gaps and requested decisions; do not send it to others without authorization.
