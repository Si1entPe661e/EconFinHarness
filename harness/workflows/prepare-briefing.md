# Prepare a briefing or decision memo

Use `briefing-writing-efh` to identify what the audience must know or decide. Establish actual status with `project-status-efh` when relevant. Select evidence, preserve essential qualifications and state recommendations, viable alternatives and next actions.

For slides, build a narrative with one purpose per slide and use the available presentation skill for the actual artifact. For a short written update, keep the medium simple. Run the [revise module](revise-writing.md) on the draft unless its skip conditions apply. It keeps the alternatives, risks, blockers and status a decision needs while removing defensive wording and logic gaps. Deliver the briefing, its material evidence gaps and requested decisions; do not send it to others without authorization.
