# Review research claims

Identify the claim and follow its method, sample, inference and output through the [composition guide](../skill-composition.md). The `reviewer` responsibility applies to the requested review or a concrete unresolved issue.

Compare the estimand and assumptions with the fitted sample, uncertainty, tables, figures and prose. Use the relevant main method, `code-audit`, `panel-data-checks`, `inference-clustering` and `tables-figures`.

Flag claims that exceed their support: associations interpreted causally, local/complier effects generalized to a population, training accuracy presented as held-out performance, or pointwise intervals described as simultaneous bands. Route formatting issues to [presentation](present-results.md) and research choices to the relevant [analysis](empirical-analysis.md) step.

Deliver focused findings and necessary revisions, stating what cannot be assessed.
